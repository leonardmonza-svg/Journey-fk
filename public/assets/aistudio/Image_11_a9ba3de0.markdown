## Brand & Style

This design system embodies soft luxury, high academic credibility, and empathetic guidance tailored for elite university admissions (PTN) and medical faculty pathways (Fakultas Kedokteran). Moving intentionally away from noisy, commercialized tutoring aesthetics, the visual language draws inspiration from ethereal glassmorphism, luminous ambient gradients, and soft neo-morphic depth.

The target audience encompasses determined high school achievers, gap-year medical candidates, and discerning parents seeking institutional trust, white-glove mentoring, and proven track records. The interface evokes peace of mind, high prestige, modern clarity, and intellectual vitality.

Visual pillars:
- **Soft Glassmorphism & Neo-Depth:** Multi-layered translucent card surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`), subtle inner specular borders, and expansive, pastel lilac-to-violet atmospheric light fields.
- **Deep Botanical Violet Contrast:** Anchored with deep indigo/royal purple typography (#2E1065 / #1E1B4B) to guarantee crisp legibility, academic gravity, and executive authority.
- **Luminous Violet Accents:** Strategic electric lilac and violet accents used for pathway tabs, conversion triggers, success metrics, and status badges.

## Colors

The palette balances ethereal pastel backdrops with deep, commanding ink tones. 

- **Primary (`#7C3AED` - Royal Violet):** The interactive engine. Powers primary buttons, active pathway states, key stat highlights, and focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#2E1065` - Deep Night Purple):** The primary text and structural brand anchor. Replaces harsh blacks with rich chromatic authority, evoking medical prestige and academic rigor.
- **Tertiary (`#C084FC` - Luminous Lilac):** Soft accent layer used for pill badge fills, icon container tints, subtle glowing halos, and progress track fills.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F7FC` - Alabaster Mist):** A tinted neutral base that prevents pure white screen glare and creates high optical resonance for translucent white cards.

### Extended Tokens & Overlays
- **Surface Card Glass:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- **Glass Specular Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` top/left edge to `rgba(216, 180, 254, 0.35)` bottom/right edge
- **Secondary Body Text:** `#475569` (Slate 600) for tertiary notes, and `#3B0764` (Deep Tyrian) for high-emphasis subheads
- **Success Mint / PTN Qualified:** `#10B981` (Emerald) blended with soft lilac halos

## Typography

The typography is rendered exclusively in Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver geometric warmth, contemporary precision, and effortless readability. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set tight with deliberate negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) in semi-bold and extra-bold weights. Always styled in Secondary Deep Violet (`#2E1065`) to establish authoritative focal points.
- **Metric Displays:** Numbers indicating acceptance rates, faculty passing scores, and timeline milestones use `label-stat` or `headline-lg` with tight numeric proportional figures.
- **Body & Longform:** Styled in deep neutral violet (`#3B0764` or `#475569`) with generous line-height (`1.6` ratio) to support unstrained reading across medical curriculum pathways and consultation packages.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a centered, fluid 12-column grid constrained to a maximum width of `1280px` for desktop viewports, 8 columns on tablet devices (`768px - 1024px`), and 4 columns on mobile devices (`<768px`).

Key structural conventions:
- **Section Rhythm:** Generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl` scaled to `5rem` for major section paddings) to project an unhurried, luxury academic environment.
- **Card Clustering:** Micro-groups, such as mentor avatars, curriculum milestones, and admission stats, utilize `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps within glass containers to form cohesive informational modules.
- **Edge Restraint:** Desktop gutters of `1.5rem` ensure that multi-column layouts like doctor-mentor profiles and curriculum paths remain cleanly compartmentalized without visual collision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent optical layering, multi-point atmospheric drop shadows, and delicate specular borders rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Level 1 (Ambient Glass Tile):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`
  - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(160%)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.06), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(46, 16, 101, 0.04)`
- **Level 2 (Hovered Glass / Interactive Modal):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`
  - Blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.5)`
  - Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.14), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(46, 16, 101, 0.06)`
- **Level 3 (Ethereal Highlight / Hero Asset Halo):**
  - Ambient back-glow created using a blurred radial gradient: `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(192, 132, 252, 0.4) 0%, rgba(248, 247, 252, 0) 70%)` situated directly behind hero imagery, glass cards, and medical pathway trackers.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a continuous rounded geometry that feels tactile, contemporary, and deeply approachable:

- **Base Radius (Roundedness 2):** Standard inputs, cards, and modal sheets use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to create organic, smooth contours that harmonize with soft glass surfaces.
- **Badge & Chip Enclosures:** Always fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to distinguish categorical tags, university logos, and pathway indicators from structural cards.
- **Interactive Action Elements:** Primary and secondary buttons utilize `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`) for a balanced, premium tactile feel under touch and cursor states.
